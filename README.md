# Clean Air & Clean Street

A full-stack municipal governance platform designed for environmental incident reporting, live tracking, and district patrol management. Developed by team **Gen Z Coders**.

---

## 🚀 What It Does
**Clean Air & Clean Street** bridges the gap between citizens and municipal authorities. It empowers everyday people to take active control of their local environment by reporting pollution, waste, and street maintenance issues. Behind the scenes, the platform leverages automated AI categorization to route issues directly to the appropriate patrol crews, ensuring fast response times, transparent tracking, and data-driven urban cleanliness management.

---

## 🛠️ What Is Included (Features & Architecture)

### 1. Citizen Report Hub
* **Geolocated Incident Logging**: Submit real-time reports detailing air quality degradation or street cleanliness issues with precise location pinning.
* **Proof Verification**: Mandatory image upload fields to ensure validity and prevent spam.
* **Community Upvoting**: A public ledger that lets citizens upvote critical regional issues to push them higher up the municipal priority list.

### 2. Patrol & Administration Hub
* **Live Incident Tracking**: An interactive dashboard showing active cases, pending status updates, and resolved issues across districts.
* **Crew Dispatching**: Tools for departmental managers to assign patrol crews and monitor field progress.
* **Analytics Dashboard**: Regional metrics tracking environmental health over time.

### 3. Intelligent Automation
* **Google Gemini API Integration**: Automatically analyzes submitted text and image descriptions to categorize incidents, assess severity levels, and suggest appropriate response vectors.
